Start With The Symptom

What Your Tankless Heater Is Telling You

Owners rarely say the heat exchanger is scaled. They say the water is lukewarm, the display shows a code, the unit clicks and stops or hot water takes forever to arrive. Each symptom narrows the cause.

A code or a tripped breaker usually points to a safety shutdown. Lukewarm output with no code often means reduced flow or scale. A cold gap between two hot draws is usually a flow-rate or setup issue and not a failure.

Local Water

Why Scale Hits Tankless Units Hard in South Florida

Hollywood's water comes from the Biscayne Aquifer, a limestone and sand formation. The city treats it with lime and ferric chloride to reduce hardness and color, but dissolved minerals can still build up inside a tankless unit that heats water quickly.

A thin layer of scale on the heat exchanger acts as insulation. The unit works harder, output drops and codes appear. Many owners see the first sign in the second or third year if no one has flushed it.

Salt air near Hollywood Beach and the Intracoastal also corrodes outdoor vent terminations and gas connections, so check them when the unit is serviced. A replacement or relocation must meet the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Questions

Tankless Water Heater Questions

Many manufacturers call for a yearly flush, and hard water or heavy use can justify more often. Check the manual for your model, because skipping it is a common reason for error codes.

Codes usually point to scale, a blocked vent or intake, a flow problem or a failed sensor. The code is a clue and not a diagnosis, so a technician confirms the cause.

Short draws can fall below the minimum flow the unit needs, and a scaled heat exchanger can reduce output. Flow settings, scale and recirculation setups are the first things to check.

Most problems are fixable, especially when the unit is under about fifteen years old. If the heat exchanger has failed on an older unit, planned water heater replacement compares options.

Hard water scales the heat exchanger, so a softener or scale prevention often extends the unit's life. See the water softener page for how softeners work in this area.

Leave the house, then call 911 and the gas utility from outside. Do not use switches or flames. A technician can service the unit after the gas company clears it.
