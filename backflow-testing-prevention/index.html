The Local Rule

What Hollywood's City Code Says About Backflow Testing

Section 51.210 of the City of Hollywood code, adopted in Ordinance O-2015-29 on December 2, 2015, requires every mandatory backflow prevention assembly to be professionally tested at least annually. The customer is responsible for hiring a certified backflow assembly tester and for submitting the results to the Building Division.

The same section lets the utility interrupt water service to the private system if the documentation is missing. For a restaurant or an apartment building, a shutoff is a far bigger cost than the test.

The state rule behind local programs is Chapter 62-555 of the Florida Administrative Code, which sets cross-connection control standards for public water systems.

Check the date on your last report now. If it has passed a year, schedule the test before the city asks.

What You Might Notice

Signs a Backflow Device Needs Attention

Most owners learn about a failed device from a notice or a test. These signs mean test sooner:

A city letter or tag

A notice that a device is due, overdue or failed its last test.

Water dripping at the relief port

A steady drip from an RPZ assembly can mean a failed check valve.

Dirty water from sprinkler heads

Cloudy or discolored water in an irrigation zone.

A new irrigation system

New systems need a backflow preventer and a first test.

Frost or storm damage

Impact and weather crack bodies and test cocks.

Safety

Why It Matters

Why a Check Valve Is Worth Testing Every Year

A backflow preventer sits idle most of the year. Rubber seals harden, springs weaken and debris lodges on a seat, and nothing looks wrong from outside. The test is the only way to learn a valve is not sealing.

South Florida adds its own wear. Constant irrigation, sandy soil that finds its way into valve bodies and summer storms that spike line pressure all work against these assemblies, so waiting for a visible problem is a poor test.

Restaurants and shops in Pembroke Park and across Hollywood fall under the same annual test, and the commercial plumbing page covers the wider checklist. If a vehicle or fence work breaks the assembly body, water leaks onto the ground and the device needs repair or replacement. When the water source is unclear, leak detection testing separates a damaged assembly from a leak elsewhere on the supply line.
