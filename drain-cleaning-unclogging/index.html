A Costly Shortcut

Why Store-Bought Cleaners Cost More Than They Save

Bottled drain openers work by heat and caustic chemistry. In a pipe that is already stressed, that same chemistry can soften a plastic trap, pit an old metal fitting and leave a pool of corrosive liquid above a blockage it never moved.

When a technician then opens the line, that liquid is waiting. Tell the dispatcher if you used any product, because it changes the gloves, the tools and the order of work.

Cable machines clear most household clogs by cutting through the blockage rather than dissolving it, and a camera shows what is left on the pipe wall afterward.

Hollywood Homes

What Clogs Look Like in an Older Hollywood House

Houses built after the city's 1925 founding, including many streets around Hollywood Lakes and downtown, often have original cast iron drain pipe. Cast iron rusts on the inside and narrows over time, so scale catches paper and grease long before the pipe fails.

Mature landscaping adds another cause. Roots follow moisture into a joint, and the first sign is a toilet that gurgles or a tub that drains slowly after rain. Roots grow back from the cut ends, and older streets such as the 1960s blocks of Pembroke Pines see the same pattern, so cabling alone often repeats, and hydro jetting service is the next step to clear the walls.

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC), requires accessible cleanouts in a building drain system. Knowing where yours sits saves time on an emergency visit.

If the camera shows a cracked or collapsed section, the repair question moves to sewer line repair service, and you see the footage before deciding.

The Kitchen

Kitchen Drains: Grease, Starch and the Disposal

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom drains. Grease cools and coats the pipe wall, rice and pasta swell into starch plugs, and a disposal pushes fine solids into a line that was not sized for them.

Run hot water before and after the disposal, scrape plates into the trash and keep cooking fat out of the sink. A kitchen line that clogs every few months needs a cleaning that scours the wall, which is a job for a cable with the right head or high-pressure hydro jetting.

The Bathroom

Bathroom Drains: Hair, Soap Scum and Toilet Paper

Hair mats around the stopper and soap scum narrows the pipe behind it, so a tub that drains slowly is usually a hair clog within arm's reach of the drain. A hair catcher and a monthly clean prevent most of them.

If only the toilet is slow, the clog sits in the toilet itself or just past it. If the tub gurgles when the toilet flushes, the two share a branch line and the clog is farther down, which is a cable job and not a plunger job.

Limits Of A Cable

When a Cable Is Not Enough

A cable clears a blockage but cannot repair a pipe. If the same line clogs again within months, or the cable stops at the same spot each time, the cause is probably roots, a sag or a broken joint.

That is the point to look inside the pipe with a camera. What the camera finds moves the job from cleaning to sewer lateral repair, and it is better to know before the next weekend backup.

Shared Lines

Shared Drains in Apartments, Plazas and Condos

A clog in a shared stack or a plaza's main drain is not only your problem. Water backs up at the lowest fixture on the line, which is often a ground-floor unit or a restaurant, and the cause may be several floors or several tenants upstream.

Tell building management before the visit, because shared lines usually need a coordinated cleaning. For restaurants and food-service tenants, grease is the usual culprit, and commercial plumbing covers grease lines and interceptor service.

Around Hollywood

Clogs Near Downtown, the Beach and the Lakes

Restaurants and shops around downtown Hollywood and Young Circle put grease into drain lines faster than a home does, and a grease clog behaves differently from a root clog. The beach side adds seasonal crowds in rentals, which means heavy use and the wrong things flushed.

Inland, houses near Hollywood Lakes and Hillcrest more often fight roots in older laterals. A camera pass tells you which kind of clog you have.
