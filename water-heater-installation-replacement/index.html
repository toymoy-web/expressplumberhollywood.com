The Decision

Repair, Replace or Upgrade?

If the tank leaks from the body, replacement is the only answer. If the unit is under ten years old and the problem is an element or thermostat, water heater diagnosis and repair usually makes more sense. Past ten years with repairs stacking up, a new unit often costs less over the next five years.

A planned replacement beats an emergency one. You compare prices, pick the model and schedule the permit instead of paying an after-hours rate.

What a Code-Compliant Installation Includes

  • ✓A plumbing permit and inspection, which Florida cities typically require
  • ✓A temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge line to a safe location
  • ✓A drain pan with a drain line where a leak could damage the structure
  • ✓Proper venting for gas units, and an electrical disconnect for electric units
  • ✓In a garage, gas ignition sources raised above the floor under the fuel gas code
  • ✓An expansion tank where the system is closed and pressure rises

South Florida Factors

Hard Water, Salt Air and Where You Put the Heater

Hollywood's water comes from the Biscayne Aquifer. The city treats it with lime to reduce hardness, but scale can still collect, and a yearly flush plus an anode rod check every few years protects a new unit.

Salt air near the coast corrodes connectors and the tank jacket, and garage heaters sit in summer heat. When choosing a model, ask about corrosion-resistant fittings and a drain pan that is kept dry.

If you are replacing a heater that failed from a leak, ask for a water leak detection check so you know the water did not come from a hidden line. Miramar homes range from older to brand new, and the Miramar page explains how age changes the plan.
