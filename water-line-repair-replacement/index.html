The Basics

What the Service Line Is and Who Owns It

The water service line runs underground from the utility's meter to your house. In most places, the utility owns the pipe up to and including the meter, and the property owner owns the line from there. Ask Hollywood Public Utilities to confirm where your responsibility starts.

Because the pipe is buried, a failure is hard to spot. A pinhole can leak for months and show up first as a higher bill.

Replacement pipe must be a material approved under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and many jobs need a permit and inspection.

Emergency Water Line

Emergency Water Line Repair: Water in the Yard or No Water in the House

Two calls about the buried line come in more than any others. One is water coming up out of the ground, a soft wet patch or a small swamp in the yard. The other is a house with no water at all, or with only a trickle.

Close the house valve and check the meter. If the meter still turns, the leak sits between the meter and the house, and the yard may show it before your bill does. If the meter has stopped and the house still has no water, the cause may be a closed valve, a failed fitting inside or a supply issue the utility has to handle.

A leak right at the meter box needs a quick question to the utility about which side it sits on, so call both. Do not dig around a meter box on your own.

The meter counts every gallon, including the ones that leak into the ground. One rate calculator built on the city's fiscal 2026 rates puts extra water at about $15.59 per 1,000 gallons on the combined water and sewer bill. Roots, Coral Rock and Sandy Soil

South Florida's sandy soil shifts, and limestone rock lies close to the surface in places, which can abrade buried pipe over decades. Tree roots and yard work add risk to shallow lines, and a high water table makes digging messier.

Buried Water Lines Under Hollywood Lawns and Streets

The line from the meter to the house runs under the front lawn or driveway on most streets, from Hollywood Lakes to Emerald Hills. In sandy South Florida soil, a leak can drain away without a wet patch.

Close the main valve at the house and watch the meter. If it keeps moving, the loss is on the buried side, and locating it comes before any digging.

Low Pressure

Low Pressure: One Fixture, One Floor or the Whole House

Weak flow at one faucet usually points to the aerator or the supply valve, and weak flow in one bathroom points to that branch. Weak flow everywhere points to the pressure regulator, a leak or the line from the street.

Test a hose bib outside and compare it with an indoor tap. A strong hose bib with weak indoor taps suggests the house piping, while weak flow at both suggests the service line, a partly closed valve or the utility side.

If your neighbors are also affected, call the utility first. If only your house is weak, call us and say whether it started suddenly or got worse over time.
