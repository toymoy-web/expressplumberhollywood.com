The Town

Florida's Largest Town, Bordering Hollywood and Dania Beach

Davie is a town of 105,691 people in the 2020 census, which makes it the most populous town in Florida. It spreads over 35.76 square miles, most of it land, and it was incorporated on November 16, 1925, after settlement began in 1909.

Robert Parsell Davie, a businessman, bought about 44 square miles of reclaimed swampland and developed it, and the community that had been called Zona took his name. Today the town hosts Nova Southeastern University and a Florida Atlantic University campus.

Septic or Sewer

Why the First Question Is Septic or Sewer

Some Davie streets are on sewer and some are not, and the equipment changes with the answer. A backup on a septic home usually means a full tank or a wet drain field, and the first call is a pump-out and inspection.

On a sewer home, the same symptom points to a blocked lateral, which a cable machine and a camera can clear and show. About 44 percent of neighboring Hollywood relies on septic, so the dispatcher asks early. Sewer backup service covers both cases. Repairs follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Large Lots

Long Yard Lines, Pools and Irrigation

Some Davie properties carry more yard than a typical subdivision lot. A larger yard means longer irrigation lines, pool supply lines and outdoor spigots, and a small leak in a buried run can drain away into sandy soil without a puddle.

The meter test separates an inside leak from a buried one. Close the main valve at the house, and if the meter keeps moving, the loss is underground, which is a job for leak detection and sometimes water line repair.
