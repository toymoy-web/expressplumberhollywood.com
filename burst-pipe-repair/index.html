The Fix

How a Burst Pipe Is Repaired

The crew shuts off and drains the line, cuts out the failed section and replaces it with new pipe of the right material and size. Press fittings, soldered joints or push-to-connect couplings are chosen to match the existing pipe and the code.

Afterward, the line is pressure tested and checked for other weak points. If one section has burst because of age, the pipe next to it may be ready to follow. We point out suspect sections so you can decide with the facts.

Pipe Materials

Copper, CPVC and PVC: Repairing the Pipe You Actually Have

A cracked PVC line, a pinhole in older copper and a split CPVC section are different repairs. PVC cracks from sun, impact or soil movement and is cut and spliced with new fittings. Copper develops pinholes and corrosion, and the bad section is replaced. CPVC turns brittle with age and can split along its length.

We identify the material before cutting, because mixing pipe types without the right fitting leads to the next leak. If the same run has failed twice, the repair usually turns into a talk about replacing it.

Ask for a pressure test before the wall is closed, and photograph the pipe, the fitting and the gauge for your records.
