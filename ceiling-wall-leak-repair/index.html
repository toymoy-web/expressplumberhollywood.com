Emergency Leaks

Emergency Ceiling and Wall Leaks: Water Coming Through Right Now

Water coming through a ceiling is an emergency. It follows the framing before it shows, it soaks insulation and it can bring drywall down. Close the main valve if you suspect a pipe, then set a bucket under the drip and keep people out from under the stain.

The usual emergency sources are the bathroom above, a leaking supply or drain inside a wall, a second-floor balcony and an attic line. Tell the dispatcher which room the water appears in and what sits above it.

When the source is unclear, we check the plumbing first and then point you to an AC or roofing contractor if the plumbing is dry. A dated written finding helps your insurer and your landlord or association.

Why Water Hides

How a Leak Travels Before It Shows

Water follows framing and pipes before it appears on a surface. A stain on the ceiling can be several feet from the real source, since water runs along joists and drywall seams until it finds a low spot.

A moisture meter and a thermal camera map where the wall or ceiling is wet and where it is dry. The source usually sits at the edge of the wet zone nearest the pipe.

Because South Florida is humid, a damp cavity grows mold quietly and drywall can stay wet for a long time. Repairs to supply and drain piping follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). If the wall is already soft, electronic leak detection finds the pipe, and repair follows.

Attics, Second Floors and Condo Neighbors

Many Hollywood houses are single story on slabs, but newer two-story homes and condos run supply lines through ceilings and walls. Water heaters in attics and closets add risk, because a slow leak can soak insulation and drywall before anyone notices. For a failing tank, water heater repair visit covers the options.

In condo towers, a ceiling leak often starts in the unit above. Ask management where the nearest riser valve is, and read the ownership rules on the Hallandale Beach page.
