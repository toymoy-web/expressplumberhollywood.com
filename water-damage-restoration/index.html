The First Hour

Emergency Steps Before the Crew Arrives

  • 1Stop the source. Close the main water valve or the fixture shutoff.
  • 2Turn off power to wet rooms at the breaker if you can reach it safely. Never stand in water near outlets.
  • 3Photograph everything before you move it.
  • 4Lift furniture legs off wet carpet and move valuables, papers and electronics to dry areas.
  • 5Open windows only if outdoor humidity is lower than indoor. In South Florida, closed doors with the AC running often work better.

How Restoration Is Planned

Water Categories Decide the Plan

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration sorts water by contamination level, and a restoration plan is built around those categories.

CategorySourceWhat it means for materials
Category 1Supply line, appliance overflow, rain intrusionDry in place when caught early
Category 2Washing machine or dishwasher discharge, toilet overflow with urine onlyDry carefully, remove porous items such as carpet pad
Category 3Sewage backup, rising floodwaterRemove porous materials, clean and disinfect

The category can worsen with time. Clean water that sits for more than a day or two can become Category 2, so speed lowers both cost and risk.

South Florida Factor

Why Humidity Makes Drying Harder Here

Hollywood sits on the Atlantic coast between Fort Lauderdale and Miami, with an average high of about 83 degrees and a low near 68. Warm, moist air slows evaporation, so drying equipment has to pull more water from the air than it would in a dry climate.

Wet drywall, baseboard and carpet pad can hold moisture for days. Air movers push dry air across surfaces, and dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the room. The crew takes moisture readings each visit and keeps the equipment running until the readings match dry reference values.

Hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30, and storm-driven rain is a common source of roof and window intrusion in this area. Condo losses in Hallandale Beach add shared-wall rules on top of the drying plan.

Fix the Cause

Water Damage Often Starts With a Plumbing Failure

Restoration dries the structure. It does not repair the pipe that failed. A burst supply line, a leaking tank or a backed-up sewer all need plumbing repair, and drying a house with an active leak only restarts the problem.

If the water came from the water heater, emergency water heater repair handles the unit. A sewage source starts with emergency sewer line repair, and a mystery wet spot starts with leak detection.

Around Hollywood

Water Damage in the Condos and Houses of Hollywood

A leak in a Hollywood Beach tower can spread through ceilings and walls in several units before anyone finds the source. In a house in Emerald Hills or Hillcrest, the water more often stays on one level but can sit under flooring.

In both cases the early hours matter, because humidity in South Florida speeds mold growth. Document the damage before cleanup, and tell the crew which kind of building it is.
