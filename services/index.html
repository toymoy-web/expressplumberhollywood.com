How to Choose the Right Service

Most calls start with a symptom and not a service name. A meter that moves with every tap closed points to leak detection. Every drain slowing at once in a septic home points to pumping. One slow sink points to a drain cleaning visit, and a drain that clogs again within weeks points to jetting and a camera look.

Hollywood adds its own twist. About 44 percent of the city still runs on septic, so a backup can be a tank problem and not a pipe problem. Tell the dispatcher if your home is on septic or city sewer when you call, and the dispatcher plans the visit around it.

What to Do in a Gas or Electrical Emergency

  • ✓Smell gas: leave the house and call 911 and the gas utility from outside.
  • ✓Water near live outlets or an electrical panel: leave the room and cut power only if you can reach the breaker without standing in water.
  • ✓Carbon monoxide alarm sounding: get everyone outside and call 911.

Plumbing work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, a standard built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and the repair visit starts once the danger has passed.
