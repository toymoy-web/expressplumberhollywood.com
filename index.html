Quick Triage

What Are You Seeing? Find Your First Move

Pick the symptom closest to yours. Because 44 percent of Hollywood relies on septic tanks, the same symptom can mean a tank problem in one house and a city sewer problem in the next, so each answer says what to check first.

Likely a full septic tank or a main-line blockage

On septic, slow drains in every fixture usually point to a full tank or a saturated drain field. On city sewer, the same pattern points to a blockage in the main line. First move: stop running water, then find out which system you have. Start with septic tank pumping or sewer line repair.

Likely a hidden leak

Water is leaving the pipes somewhere. First move: close the main valve and watch the meter. If it stops, the leak is inside the house. If it keeps moving, the leak is on the buried line. See leak detection or slab leak detection and repair.

Likely a failed heater or a leaking tank

Cut power at the breaker or set the gas control to off, then close the cold supply valve above the tank. A leak from the tank body means replacement. Start with water heater repair or water heater installation and replacement.

Likely a blocked lateral or a system surge

First move: stop all water use and keep people away from the wet area. A camera shows whether the blockage is in your pipe or in the main. See sewer backup for the emergency steps or emergency sewer line repair for the fix.

Stop the water first

Close the main shutoff, open the lowest faucet to drain the line and cut power to wet outlets only if it is safe. Then call. See burst pipe repair for the repair, and water damage restoration for the drying.

Check the cheap causes first

A dry trap in an unused drain is the most common cause, and running water for a minute often fixes it. A smell outdoors near the yard points to the septic tank. If it persists, see drain cleaning or septic tank pumping and inspection.

Local Fact That Changes Your Call

Nearly Half of Hollywood Is Still on Septic

The City of Hollywood's Public Utilities Department says 44 percent of the city still relies on septic tanks, and about a third of the septic tanks left in Broward County sit inside Hollywood. In July 2026 the city commission approved a rate plan to fund a 25-year project that replaces those tanks with central sewer, with construction starting in 2032.

For a homeowner, that means a backup is often a tank or drain-field problem, not a city main problem, and the fix starts with pumping and inspection. Waiting for the sewer conversion does not make today's tank last longer. Most households pump every three to five years, and a garbage disposal or a large family shortens that interval.

Two of these signs together are a reason to call and say "septic" first, so the dispatcher sends the right equipment:

  • ✓Gurgling in the pipes when a washing machine drains
  • ✓Slow drains in every fixture at once, not just one sink
  • ✓A sewage smell near the tank lid or over the drain field
  • ✓Grass that stays soggy or unusually green above the field after dry weather

The full process, from locating the lid to checking baffles, is on our septic tank pumping page for Hollywood.

Storms And Sewers

After a Heavy Rain, Sewer Backups Come First

When Tropical Storm Eta soaked South Florida in November 2020, rainwater overwhelmed the regional plant that treats sewage for Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Dania Beach, Hallandale Beach, Miramar and Pembroke Park. City officials said the plant was handling about 100 million gallons a day against a normal 45 to 50 million, and sewage bubbled up through manholes in East Hollywood.

Heavy rain loads the same system your house drains into. The lowest fixtures, usually a first-floor shower, a tub or a floor drain, push water back first. If that happens, stop running water, skip the plunger on sewage and call.

A camera inspection of the lateral separates a blockage in your own pipe from a surge in the main, which decides whether the repair is yours or the utility's. Our sewer line repair crews start with that camera pass.

Older Homes Add Their Own Risk

Houses built in the decades after the city's 1925 founding, including many around Hollywood Lakes and downtown, often still have original cast iron drain lines. Cast iron rusts from the inside, narrows over time and cracks at the joints. The first sign is usually a drain that slows again a few months after it was cleared.

That pattern is the cue to ask about hydro jetting plus a camera look, which tells you whether the pipe needs cleaning, a spot repair or replacement before a full backup forces the decision.

Water And Salt Air

Coastal Water Is Hard on Heaters and Fixtures

Hollywood's tap water is drawn from the Biscayne Aquifer, a limestone and sand formation roughly 50 to 200 feet underground. Water that moves through limestone picks up calcium, which settles as scale on heating elements and in the bottom of tanks. Salt air near Hollywood Beach and the Intracoastal also speeds corrosion on exposed supply lines, shutoff valves and outdoor fittings.

Small habits prevent the 2 a.m. call. Flush a tank water heater once a year, have the anode rod checked around year three, and replace a corroded angle stop before it fails behind a vanity.

When a unit is already leaking from the tank itself, emergency water heater repair usually ends in a replacement. The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is built on the International Plumbing Code, is the standard a permitted installation must meet, including the relief valve and drain.

Before The Plumber Arrives

What To Do in the First Ten Minutes

  • 1Close the main water valve. If the leak is at one toilet or sink, close that fixture's shutoff instead.
  • 2Leaking water heater: cut power at the breaker or turn the gas control to off, then close the cold supply valve above the tank.
  • 3Sewage backing up: stop using sinks, showers, toilets and the washing machine, because every gallon goes where the blockage is.
  • 4Skip chemical drain cleaners. They can damage pipes and burn the person who opens the line.
  • 5Document before you clean. Photograph the damage and the failed part, then move rugs, electronics and papers off wet floors.
  • 6Smell gas? Leave the house and call 911 and the gas utility first. A plumber handles the repair afterward.
