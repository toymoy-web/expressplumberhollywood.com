The Decision

Repair, Lining or Replacement: Where the Line Gets Drawn

A single crack in an otherwise good pipe is a repair. A pipe with roots at several joints may be a candidate for lining. A pipe with a collapsed section, a long belly or widespread cracking is a replacement.

The camera decides. You see the video, we explain what each defect means, and you choose with prices in front of you. Most owners are surprised how often sewer line repair work is enough, and how clearly the footage shows when it is not.

Local Pipe

What Ages Out Under Hollywood Lots

Many Hollywood houses built in the decades after the city's 1925 founding have cast iron or clay laterals. Cast iron rusts inward and cracks at joints, and clay opens at its hubs. By sixty or seventy years, a lateral can be the weakest part of the house.

Mature landscaping adds roots that find the joints, and a high water table keeps the soil wet around the pipe. Heavy summer rain then loads the line.

Because the city's regional plant takes sewage from Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Dania Beach, Hallandale Beach, Miramar and Pembroke Park, heavy storms can stress the whole system at once, which is why a sound lateral matters.
