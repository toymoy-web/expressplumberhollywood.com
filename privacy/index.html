Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026. This policy explains what information Hollywood Express Plumbing collects when you use this site or call (954) 697-4920.

What we collect

This site has no forms, accounts or comment sections. The information we receive comes from two places: phone calls to (954) 697-4920 and emails to contact@expressplumberhollywood.com.

When you call, our telephone and call-tracking systems record the caller's number, the date, time and length of the call, and the page or source that led to it. The dispatcher also notes the details you give about the plumbing problem and your address so the visit can be planned. If a call is recorded or monitored for quality, you are told at the start of the call.

When you email us, we keep the message and your address so we can reply.

How we use it

We use call and email information to answer your request, route the job to a plumbing professional, confirm the price and schedule the visit, to measure which pages lead to calls and to meet legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with the plumbing provider handling your job and with service providers that support our phone and email systems.

Third-party services on this site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the coverage map is an embedded Google Map. Those services may receive your IP address and technical details, under Google's own privacy policy. This site does not currently run advertising or analytics cookies of its own.

Links to other sites, including government pages and standards bodies, are governed by those sites' policies.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing contact@expressplumberhollywood.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify who you are first.

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions go to contact@expressplumberhollywood.com or 1701 Park St, Suite 22, Hollywood, FL.

Questions about this page? Call (954) 697-4920 or email contact@expressplumberhollywood.com.
