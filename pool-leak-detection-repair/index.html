Do This First

The Bucket Test Anyone Can Run

  • 1Fill a bucket with pool water to about an inch from the top.
  • 2Set it on a step so the bucket water level matches the pool level, and mark both.
  • 3Leave the pump and auto-fill off for 24 hours.
  • 4Compare the drops. A larger pool drop than bucket drop suggests a leak.
  • 5Repeat with the pump running to see whether the leak is in the plumbing.

Real estate listings show hundreds of Hollywood homes with pools. On one listing site, hundreds of homes with pools were for sale in the West Hollywood area alone, and many more in Hollywood Hills and Emerald Hills. Pools are part of ordinary South Florida housing.
