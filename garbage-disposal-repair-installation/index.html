Safe First Steps

What To Try Before You Call

  • 1Switch off the disposal and unplug it or flip its breaker.
  • 2Look inside with a flashlight, and remove any visible object with tongs, never a hand.
  • 3Insert a hex wrench in the bottom socket and work it back and forth to free the motor.
  • 4Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit.
  • 5Restore power and run cold water while testing.

Local Fact

The Septic Question in Hollywood

About 44 percent of Hollywood still relies on septic tanks, according to the city's Public Utilities Department. A disposal sends food solids into the tank, so septic owners do best when they use it lightly and skip fats, bones and fibrous scraps.

If your drains slow in every fixture after you started using a disposal heavily, the tank may be filling. Pumping and an inspection tell you the real condition. Our septic tank pumping visits begin with a look at the tank's sludge level.

Choosing a Unit

Horsepower, Feed Type and Noise

A one-third horsepower unit handles light use, while one-half to one horsepower suits heavier cooking. Continuous-feed units run while the switch is on, and batch-feed units run only with the stopper in place.

Sound insulation reduces noise, which matters in open kitchens and condos. Stainless grind components last longer than cheaper alternatives, especially with Hollywood's mineral-rich water.

A disposal that leaks at the sink connection also affects the cabinet below, so faucet and sink repair often goes along with a disposal visit. Condo owners in Hallandale Beach should check building rules on kitchen work.

Connections

The Dishwasher Connection and the Knockout Plug

A disposal has a dishwasher inlet with a knockout plug inside it. If the plug is not removed when a dishwasher is connected, the dishwasher will not drain, which is a common reason for a drain complaint right after a new disposal goes in.

The dishwasher hose should loop high under the counter before it reaches the disposal so dirty sink water cannot flow back. Ask the installer to show you both when the job is done.

Around Hollywood

Disposals in Septic and Sewer Neighborhoods of Hollywood

Whether a disposal is a good idea depends on what serves the house. Sewer-served homes anywhere from downtown to the western neighborhoods can run one normally, and septic homes should use one sparingly.

If you are unsure which you have, confirm it before installing. A disposal adds solids to a septic tank and shortens the time between pumpings.
