Why It Happens

The Usual Causes of a Sewer Backup

Roots entering a joint, grease that cools and hardens in the line, wipes and paper that catch on a rough spot, a collapsed section and a sag where water pools are the common causes. Heavy rain adds load, since stormwater can enter older pipe through cracks.

Older Hollywood houses often still have cast iron or clay laterals, which crack and open at joints with age. A repeat backup after cleaning is a signal to look at the pipe itself. Drain cleaning clears the blockage, and a camera shows why it formed.

Prevention

Backwater Valves and Regular Camera Checks

A backwater valve closes when flow reverses and can protect the lowest floor from a surge in the main. It needs a permit in most places, regular cleaning and a sound lateral to be useful.

A camera check of an older lateral every few years finds cracks and roots before they turn into a backup. The inspection costs less than the cleanup it can prevent. If a pit pump lifts wastewater from a lower bath, sump and ejector pump service covers it.
